# Preservation / rights note

This repository is intended for preservation, restoration, interoperability, and research on obsolete computer hardware.

The BIOS images contain historical firmware from American Megatrends/QDI and remain subject to the rights of their respective copyright holders. Their inclusion here is not a claim of ownership or an open-source license grant.

Board photographs are included as documentation of the preserved hardware.

No warranty is provided. When testing firmware, keep a verified copy of the original ROM and preferably use a separate replacement ROM rather than erasing the original device.
